\abstract{Recently, virtual assistant tools based on artificial intelligence, such as ChatGPT, gained much attention from the media and the general public. That raised questions about which kind of tasks the usage of such a tool can be applied. In the context of software development, identifying which design pattern can be appropriate for a given scenario can be considered an advanced skill that requires a high degree of abstraction and a good knowledge of object orientation. So, the goal of this paper is to perform an exploratory study investigating the effectiveness of ChatGPT in assisting developers in choosing a design pattern to solve design scenarios. To reach this goal, we gathered 50 existing questions used by teachers and public tenders that provide a concrete context and ask which design pattern would be suitable. These questions were submitted to ChatGPT, and the answers were analyzed. As a result, 96\% of the questions were answered correctly with a good level of detail, demonstrating the potential of such a tool as a valuable resource to help developers to apply design patterns and make design decisions.}

%\abstract{This study evaluates the effectiveness of ChatGPT, an AI-assisted tool capable of suggesting design patterns based on specific requirements or problems, in assisting developers in choosing a design pattern to solve a design scenario. The study gathered 50 tests from educational institutions and public tenders that require students to recognize a GoF design pattern. The results indicate that ChatGPT got 96\% of the questions right and 4\% wrong, demonstrating its potential as a valuable resource to help developers recognize and interpret design patterns. The study highlights the importance of design patterns in software development and the efforts being made to educate and train developers in their use. Overall, this study is a promising step in the development of AI-assisted tools for software development.}

\section{Introduction}\label{sec1}

The use of design patterns\cite{bib1,bib3,bib6} in software development is a practice that brings several advantages, including code reuse, better code organization, ease of maintenance, better scalability, and software quality\cite{ bib4, bib8}. However, becoming proficient in understanding and applying these patterns can take time and effort\cite{bib2}, requiring a high degree of abstraction and practice in object-oriented programming. Educational institutions use various learning techniques\cite{bib5, bib7} to engage students with real-world problems in recognizing and interpreting these patterns.

ChatGPT is an AI-assisted tool capable of answering questions in a conversational way. Recently, this kind of tool has been receiving a lot of attention from the media and the general public, raising questions about the potential of its application in several fields, such as public health \cite{biswas2023role} and academia \cite{lund2023chatting}. Some recent works suggest its use for software engineering applications, such as solving programming bugs \cite{surameery2023use} and improving other kinds of activities, like requirements engineering and code quality \cite{white2023chatgpt}.

%ChatGPT is an AI-assisted tool capable of suggesting design patterns based on specific requirements or issues. Tools like this use prior knowledge of software development and design patterns to help developers identify design patterns and analyze the situation, identifying essential requirements and context.

\textbf{ How effective are ChatGPT responses in assisting developers in discovering a design pattern to solve a design scenario?} This study aims to evaluate the effectiveness of ChatGPT responses to assist developers in choosing a design pattern to solve a design scenario for this purpose;
We gathered 50 questions from exercises or tests obtained from educational institutions and public tenders, with the prerequisite of having a context or problem and the purpose of recognizing a GoF design pattern; as a result, ChatGPT answered 96\% of the questions correctly and 4\% incorrectly.

\section{Research Methodology}\label{sec4}
% passos do estudo
In the initial phase of our research, we endeavored to identify the type of design pattern best suited for our study. We chose the catalog of 23 design patterns from the famous book “Gang of Four” (GoF); we selected the questions that make up the standards from this catalog that are widely disseminated design patterns in the job market and extensively taught in higher education or open courses.

%selecionar questões (fala do nosso critério)
In the next phase, we looked for preexisting questions prepared by higher education professors or public tenders.
We then filtered out the most pertinent questions that provided a clear context or explanation of an issue without directly referencing the pattern. 

In the final phase, we entered 50 questions into ChatGPT to determine the most appropriate design pattern for each question. We then validated whether the recommended pattern was correct or incorrect. Our dataset comprised 17 questions in English and 33 questions in Portuguese.

However, for the incorrect questions, we had 1 question in English and 2 in Portuguese, totaling three incorrect questions, as in Table~\ref{tab1}.
